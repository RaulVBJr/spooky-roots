General Plant Handling & Maintenance Guidelines

CRITICAL RULE: Do not feed any of our specimens after midnight.

1. Lighting Conditions

Most of our specimens despise standard sunlight. Be sure to carefully verify what spectrum of light your plant requires. We sell specialized illumination bulbs in every frequency (including Void and Anima radiation).

Astrological Factor: Double-check if upcoming lunar eclipses, solar eclipses, or planetary alignments affect your specimen. Certain flora experience severe mood alterations during specific zodiac transitions.

2. The Watering Ritual

For specimens that absorb liquid through standard root systems, you must speak the required incantations directly into the soil in a gentle, soothing voice. If you hear ominous whispers responding to you while watering, immediately check the water temperature or verify if you mispronounced a word in the ritual.

3. Pruning & Trimming Safety

Always wear heavy-duty leather protection. If a plant snarls, snaps, or growls at your shears, back away slowly and attempt pruning again tomorrow. Never show aggression or issue threats to flora. They retain long-term memories and will hold severe grudges.

Need Assistance? If pruning becomes impossible, email facility services and we will dispatch a certified horticulturist expeditiously.
